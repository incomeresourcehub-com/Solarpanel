The 10-Second Trick That Exposes a Lying Battery Label

Vendors love to say "200Ah lithium" and let you assume it's big. Here's how to check in your head, right there in the shop:

Battery energy (kWh) = Volts × Amp-hours ÷ 1,000
What the vendor saysWhat it really storesVerdict
"12V 200Ah lithium, it's like 5kWh"12 × 200 ÷ 1,000 = 2.4kWhLess than half
"24V 200Ah lithium"24 × 200 ÷ 1,000 = 4.8kWhAdds up
"48V 100Ah, 5kWh"48 × 100 ÷ 1,000 = 4.8kWhAdds up

That's one check. The guide gives you dozens more for batteries, panels, inverters, cables and installers, laid out as checklists you can tick on your phone.
